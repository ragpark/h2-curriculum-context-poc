How the arrangement works

One shared model of the curriculum, three things pointing at it

H2 is a governed curriculum graph with services around it. Teacher materials (H3) and learner evidence (H1) are both aligned to the same concept IDs, so a tutor can reason across them in a single context call. Click any box to see that part in action.

H3 · Teacher materials

Align the teacher's own materials to the graph, with no teacher effort

Each material is split into teaching units. The alignment service tags every unit with concepts, the teaching method, the representation and any misconceptions it addresses. The dated tags then give the class's taught-so-far set and the teacher's preferred method. Tags below 0.5 confidence go to an optional review queue (dashed outline).

H1 · Learner evidence

Turn activity into a learner model the tutor can reason with

Evidence arrives from several sources. Items already carry concept IDs. Untagged markbook entries are interpreted by the alignment service. Everything goes into an append-only store. The learner model is then rebuilt, and the graph spreads the evidence: success counts as weak evidence for prerequisites, and wrong answers are traced to the misconception that caused them.

Evaluate

Two measures. First, how accurately the alignment service tags content, checked against a hand-aligned gold set. Second, a blind A/B test of tutor replies with and without H2 context, scored by an LLM judge against the classroom ground truth. Treat the judge as indicative; confirm with teacher review.
